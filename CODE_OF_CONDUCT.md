# Code of conduct

RunicTools collaboration should be direct, respectful, and focused on the work.

- Give actionable feedback about code and outcomes, not people.
- Assume good intent while asking for evidence when correctness or safety matters.
- Do not publish private information, credentials, or security findings.
- Stop harassment, discrimination, threats, or deliberately disruptive behavior.

Organization owners are responsible for resolving conduct concerns and may limit participation when needed to protect contributors or projects.
